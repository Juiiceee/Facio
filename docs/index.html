See Facio in action.

From the full flow to the details that save you time every day.

01 — Full flow

An invoice in 30 seconds.

3 columns: my documents, the editor, the live PDF. I create an invoice, add my lines, export. No account required, no subscription.

✦ Native macOS app
02 — Crypto payments

EUR, USDC, ETH, BTC — the PDF adapts.

Some clients pay in crypto. No invoicing tool handles that. Facio supports EUR, USD, USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH natively. The PDF shows the wallet address and network. The client just has to pay.

✦ Native multi-currency
03 — Solana Pay

Payment QR code embedded in the invoice.

Your client scans → wallet opens with recipient, amount and token pre-filled. One tap to pay. Works with USDC & USDT on Solana. No copy-paste, no mistakes.

✦ Solana Pay built-in
04 — Service presets

One click = the line with the right price and VAT.

I often invoice the same services. I added presets: one click and the line appears with price and VAT pre-filled. No more copy-pasting between invoices.

✦ One-click presets
05 — Bilingual FR / EN

Each document has its own language.

French and international clients. The number, labels, and PDF — everything adapts in one click. Facture_2026_04 ↔ Invoice_2026_04

✦ French & English
06 — Time tracking

A timesheet built right into the app.

I track my hours week by week without a separate tool. It's not a complex time tracker — just what a freelancer needs: log your hours, see the total, move on.

✦ Built-in timesheet
